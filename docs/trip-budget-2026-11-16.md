# Indianapolis flight and complete trip budget

Researched November 16, 2026 UTC (November 15 in Indiana). Owner requested round-trip economy IND–SGN research and an estimate covering all trip expenses. The live homepage presents a rounded planning range, not an airfare offer or an all-inclusive tour selling price.

## Scope and itinerary

- June 1–8, 2027 tour; arrive at SGN on June 1. U.S. departure depends on flight schedule, usually one or two days earlier.
- The itinerary ends in Hoi An with included transfer to Da Nang airport (DAD). A true IND–SGN round trip requires a separate DAD–SGN flight.
- Budget allows DAD–SGN on June 8, one overnight plus additional SGN transfers, and international departure June 9. This is a routing assumption, not a held itinerary. A flight into SGN and home from DAD should also be compared; no verified multi-city fare was available, so no saving is promised.
- Per adult sharing a room, eight-traveler price of $2,875. Seven hotel nights, seven breakfasts, five lunches, three dinners, scheduled transfers, included activities and SGN–HUI are already covered. No double counting.

It anticipates airfare taxes; incremental bag/seat fees have a separate allowance.
2. Vietnam Airlines DAD–SGN route page confirms the route and shows promotional economy fares for other dates (February 2027), explicitly subject to availability and extra fees. No exact June 8 fare verified. We budget $100–$180 for a usable departure with taxes and checked baggage, rather than advertise a promotional base fare.
   https://www.vietnamairlines.com/en-vn/flights-from-da-nang-to-ho-chi-minh-city
3. Seven Corners: typical trip protection around 4–10% of trip cost; age, length, coverage and optional benefits affect premium. $200–$500 allowance for cancellation/interruption, medical and evacuation coverage is not a personalized quote. Some ages/coverage choices exceed this range.
   https://www.sevencorners.com/blog/travel-insurance-advice/what-is-travel-insurance
4. Vietnam e-visa: $25 single entry / $50 multiple entry, verified in prior task November 15 against Ministry of Public Security. Budget assumes eligible traveler applies directly, single entry.
   https://e-services.mps.gov.vn/bocongan/bothutuc/tthc?matt=26276
   https://evisa.gov.vn/

   https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/currency-and-payments-vietnam/
6. IND economy lot currently $9/day per car. About 11 parking days are $99; $120–$160 allowance includes fuel. Conservative per-person self-drive allowance; sharing a car reduces the per-person cost. Private chauffeur/taxi is outside this assumption.
   https://www.ind.com/parking/economy-lot
7. Tips $40–$60 from the existing EXO supplier guidance and group-size calculation documented in website-thoughts-2026-11-15.md; discretionary, not a mandatory surcharge.
